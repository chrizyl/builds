About Curatora

A quieter way to look closely.

Curatora is an art discovery platform for curious minds. We bring the open-access collection of The Metropolitan Museum of Art into a calm, gallery-like space — no noise, just the work and its story.

Every image and record you see is fetched live from The Met's public collection, spanning five thousand years of human creativity: from Egyptian amulets to Impressionist canvases and Japanese woodblock prints.
